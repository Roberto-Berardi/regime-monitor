The mechanism

How the position is decided

01 Why rates move asset prices Interest rates set the discount rate on future cash flows.
↓ When rates fall

Assets whose value sits far in the future are worth more.

Growth equityGold
↑ When rates rise

Assets that generate cash today are worth more.

Value equityEnergy
That sensitivity is called equity duration. Every holding is sorted from the most rate-sensitive to the least.
02 What the model reads Two observable inputs, both published daily and never revised.
The price of money
2-year Treasury yield · 3-month change

What the market expects the Fed to do next.

The quantity of money
Fed balance sheet · 13-week change

How much cash the Fed is putting into the system.

03 How the two combine A small move is noise, not a signal.
Thresholds

One Fed step on the yield, one per cent on the balance sheet. Smaller is neutral.

The tilt is a transfer, not a split: long duration gives up 6.7pp and short duration receives the same, so the risk budget stays at 55%. Method

Twenty tests, one survivor

Every variation judged against conditions fixed before it ran

Every test was judged against three conditions, fixed in writing beforehand. Setting the bar in advance is what separates a test from a search. Nineteen of the twenty failed.

01
Improve risk-adjusted return by more than 0.05 Sharpe

Anything smaller is inside the noise of a 24-year sample.

02
Hold in all three time periods

Full sample, post-crisis, last decade — not just the one that flatters it.

03
Not raise turnover by more than half

An improvement paid for in trading costs is not an improvement.

Why a rule and not a model?

A Markov model would estimate the regimes from the data, and every estimation choice is a degree of freedom a reader cannot audit. This uses five fixed numbers — two thresholds, two weights, one dwell period — all set in advance and listed above. Anyone can recompute the signal from two public FRED series.
